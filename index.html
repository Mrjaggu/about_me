01 · About

From models to platforms

I build the systems that sit between a model and a regulated business. At Axis Bank I lead AI & GenAI engineering — the platforms that turn millions of customer conversations, documents and workflows into decisions, with the governance a bank needs: PII masking, role-based access, audit trails, and controls aligned to KYC/AML.

My work spans the whole stack: self-hosted speech recognition for noisy Indic telephony audio, vLLM serving tuned for throughput on a fixed GPU budget, retrieval with guardrails, agentic workflows, and the evaluation harnesses that tell us whether any of it is actually getting better. I also write the business case — GPU economics and cost-per-call — because architecture decisions in a bank are budget decisions too.

Before Axis I was at Accenture AI, BRIDGEi2i and a run of product teams, building recommendation engines, insurance-claims extraction, and computer vision. I've been writing on Medium about applied ML since 2019, and I mentor data scientists and interns on the team.

04 · Approach

How I build

Measure first

No model change ships without an eval that agrees with human reviewers. Better means faster inference, higher recall, lower hallucination rate — in numbers.

Own the inference

In a bank, data residency decides the architecture. Self-hosted serving, tuned for the GPUs you actually have, beats a slide about the GPUs you wish you had.

Compliance is a design input

PII masking, access control and audit trails are designed in from day one — which is how you reach production with zero regulatory observations.
